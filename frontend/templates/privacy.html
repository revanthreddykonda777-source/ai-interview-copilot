Data & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains what information AI Interview Copilot collects and how it is processed.

1. Information Stored Locally

Your account and practice data are stored in the application’s local SQLite database (database.db) and uploaded files directory (uploads/):

  • Account details: Full name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Resume data: Uploaded PDF files, extracted plain text, detected skills, and your generated resume-tailored questions.
  • Practice history: Topic IDs, scores, question totals, and completion timestamps.

2. AI Processing (Google Gemini)

When you request resume feedback, question generation, answer evaluation, follow-up questions, or chat coaching:

  • Google Gemini API: Relevant text (resume text, question, or answer) is sent over HTTPS to Google’s Generative Language API using your configured API key.
  • Self-hosted Ollama (optional): The hosted site uses Google Gemini only. If you run the project yourself with Ollama installed, prompts can instead be processed by a local model on that machine.

3. Cookies & Voice Input

  • Session cookie: A standard Flask session cookie keeps you signed in and associates uploaded resumes with your account.
  • Microphone / Voice Input: Speech-to-text runs via your browser’s Web Speech API. Audio streams are not recorded or stored on our server.

4. Data Control & Caution

Avoid uploading highly sensitive personal identifiers (such as government ID numbers or banking details) inside resumes when testing with external AI APIs. You may remove uploaded PDFs from uploads/ or clear local records in database.db at any time.
