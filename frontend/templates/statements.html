Terms & Statements

Terms of use, AI coaching disclosures, and platform statements for AI Interview Copilot.

1. Terms of Use

AI Interview Copilot is provided as an interactive interview preparation and learning workspace. By using the platform, you agree to:

  • Use the practice tools, resume analyzer, and coaching features for lawful personal or educational preparation.
  • Keep your account credentials confidential and avoid attempting unauthorized access to other users’ resumes or sessions.
  • Only upload resumes and content you have the right to use.

2. AI Accuracy & Coaching Statement

Scores, ATS feedback, generated questions, follow-ups, and chat tips are produced automatically by large language models (Google Gemini or local Ollama) or similarity heuristics. They are intended solely as practice guidance—real interviewers evaluate context, culture fit, and problem-solving differently, and AI outputs may occasionally contain inaccuracies or omissions.

3. No Employment Guarantee Statement

Completing practice tracks, scoring well on mock questions, or receiving a high resume match score does not guarantee job interviews, offers, or ATS outcomes with third-party employers.

4. Availability & Service Statement

External AI availability depends on API quotas and network connectivity. When quotas are reached, the platform tries other Gemini models and then falls back to basic scoring so core practice features remain usable.
