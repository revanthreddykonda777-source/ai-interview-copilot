Frequently Asked Questions

How practice sessions, resume analysis, AI fallback, and scoring work in AI Interview Copilot.

How does AI answer evaluation work?
When you submit an answer, the app evaluates it against technical accuracy, completeness, clarity, relevance, and communication skills using Google Gemini first. Scores of 60/100 or above count as passing.
What happens if Gemini reaches its API quota or is unavailable?
AI Interview Copilot automatically switches to your local Ollama model (such as qwen3:1.7b) to handle resume analysis, question generation, answer evaluation, follow-up questions, and chat. If neither AI service is reachable, answer evaluation falls back to built-in similarity scoring so practice never stops.
How are resume-tailored interview questions generated?
Upload a PDF resume from the Resume page. The parser extracts your text and skills, provides ATS feedback, and generates 10 conversational interview questions ordered progressively from Beginner fundamentals on your skills up to Intermediate and Advanced scenarios.
Does Voice Input record or upload audio to the server?
No. Voice Input uses your browser’s built-in Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition) to transcribe speech into the text box locally in the browser. Audio files are not saved by this app.
Which resume file formats are supported?
Currently PDF (.pdf) files with selectable text are supported. Scanned image-only PDFs without embedded text cannot be parsed.
How do I save and track my progress over time?
Sign in to your account before completing a practice session. Finished sessions are recorded automatically and can be reviewed anytime on the Progress Tracker page.
